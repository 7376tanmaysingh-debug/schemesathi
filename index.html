A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions

Does SchemeSaathi decide if I qualify?

No. The finder creates a shortlist from topics you select. It does not check identity, income, location, documents, or government records. Only the scheme authority can confirm eligibility.

Is SchemeSaathi a government website?

No. SchemeSaathi is an independent guide and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government department. Official programme links are provided so you can verify details at the source.

Are all schemes listed here?

No. This is a curated starting directory, not a complete catalogue. Scheme availability and rules can change, and some benefits are administered differently by state or district.

What happens to the topics and saved schemes I choose?

Finder choices are used in this browser to filter the directory and are not sent to a server. Saved schemes are stored locally in your browser. Clearing this browser's site data removes them.

YOUR CHOICES

Privacy on SchemeSaathi

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication for your email address or phone number. SchemeSaathi does not ask for Aadhaar, income, or government account details.

What is stored here

Your saved scheme IDs are stored in this browser under your signed-in account. Finder choices are used only to filter the current page and are not saved.

Official websites

When you follow a scheme link, you leave SchemeSaathi. The destination's privacy policy and application process apply there.
